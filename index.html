How a project runs

Four steps, with a fixed price agreed before we start. Most sites launch in four to eight weeks.

  1. Review

    A free look at your current site and goals. You get a short written summary, whether or not we work together.

  2. Plan

    We agree on pages, wording and a fixed quote. You see the structure before any design begins.

  3. Design and build

    You review working pages, not just pictures, and send feedback in plain language. Two rounds of changes are included.

  4. Launch and support

    We handle the move, test on real devices and train your team. After launch, optional care plans cover updates and edits.

Questions we get asked

How much does a website cost?

It depends on the number of pages and features. After the free review we give you a fixed quote in writing, in USD or CAD, so you know the total before work starts.

How long does it take?

Most business sites launch in four to eight weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly we receive your feedback and content, and we can help write or organize that content.

Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. We build on an editor your staff can use for everyday changes like hours, staff bios and news. We train your team at launch.

Do you handle privacy and cookie requirements?

We set up cookie notices and privacy pages suited to where your customers are, including requirements in Canada and US states such as California. We are designers, not lawyers, so we recommend your own counsel reviews the final wording.

What if I already have a website?

That's the most common starting point. We review it first, tell you what's worth keeping, and plan the move so you don't lose search rankings.
