About

We’re a small web design studio that works with service businesses across the US and Canada.

Why we do this

Most service businesses are great at what they do and under-served by their website. The site is out of date, hard to use on a phone, or full of wording that doesn’t sound like them.

We build websites that fix that. We keep the process simple, speak plainly, and make sure the finished site helps a real person decide to call you.

Because we work with firms on both sides of the border, we understand the differences that matter, from accessibility rules to privacy notices to how you quote and invoice.

How we work

  • Clear over clever

    If a visitor has to think about how your site works, we’ve made it too hard.

  • Honest quotes

    You know the total before we start, and we tell you early if something won’t fit the budget.

  • Plain language

    We explain things without jargon, in emails and meetings alike.

  • Yours to keep

    You own everything we make, and you can take it elsewhere at any time.

Working across the border

We schedule calls across US and Canadian time zones, quote in USD or CAD, and build in the accessibility and privacy requirements that apply where your customers are.

We’re designers, not lawyers, so for legal wording such as privacy policies and terms we recommend that your own counsel has the final say.
