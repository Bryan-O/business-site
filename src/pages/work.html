Three concept projects showing how we approach different kinds of service businesses.

These are example projects we made up to show our thinking. They aren’t real clients. Your project will start with your own business, customers and goals.

  • Whitcombe Family Law

    Concept: Whitcombe Family Law

    Law firm

    People searching for a family lawyer are stressed and short on time. The concept puts the phone number, a plain list of practice areas and a confidential contact form on the first screen.

    • One clear next step on every page
    • Practice areas written in everyday language
    • Lawyer profiles that show experience and approach
  • Maple Row Dental

    Concept: Maple Row Dental

    Dental clinic

    New patients want to know three things: whether you take their insurance, whether you’re accepting patients, and how to book. The concept answers all three before any scrolling.

    • Online booking link on every page
    • Insurance and fees explained in one place
    • Clear directions, parking and opening hours
  • Northline Heating & Air

    Concept: Northline Heating & Air

    Heating and cooling contractor

    When a furnace stops working, nobody reads a long page. The concept leads with a tap-to-call button, service areas and an emergency request form that works on a phone.

    • Tap-to-call button always in view
    • Service areas listed by city
    • Reviews and licenses shown near the form
