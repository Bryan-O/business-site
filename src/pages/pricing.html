Pricing questions

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Most projects are split into a deposit, a payment at design approval and a final payment at launch.

Do you quote in US or Canadian dollars?

Either. We’ll quote in the currency you pay in. Applicable sales taxes such as GST/HST or state tax are added where required.

What isn’t included?

Domain registration, stock photography licenses and paid advertising are billed at cost or separately. We list them in your quote so nothing is a surprise.

What if my project is somewhere in between?

That’s normal. The three packages are starting points, and your quote will match what you actually need.
